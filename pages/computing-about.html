What we do

Learn basic coding and work on small projects together.

Computing Society is a welcoming space for students of all backgrounds to build practical programming skills at a comfortable pace. Whether you are writing your first lines of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript or collaborating with peers on a weekend mini-app, we focus on hands-on learning, friendly code reviews, and steady progress.

  • Guided sessions Step-by-step coding walkthroughs tailored for beginners.
  • Small group projects Team up with fellow students to build and demo simple tools.
  • Peer support Ask questions, debug together, and share helpful resources.
