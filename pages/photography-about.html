What we do

We are a relaxed student group for anyone who enjoys taking pictures on campus—whether you shoot with a phone or a dedicated camera.

  • Weekly campus photo walks to practice framing, light, and composition together.
  • Short beginner tips on exposure, portraits, and everyday street photography.
  • Friendly monthly photo shares where members swap feedback and favourite shots.
